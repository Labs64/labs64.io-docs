# Labs64.IO Overview

Labs64.IO is a modular platform for digital commerce products. Teams use focused services for identity, authorization, payments, checkout, auditability, and customer experiences. Services integrate through documented APIs and events; they do not share databases.

Core concepts: service, gateway, tenant, policy decision, event, sink, and contract. Incoming traffic crosses the Auth Gateway and policy layer; services own their persistence; event-producing services can send tenant-aware records through RabbitMQ to AuditFlow and configured sinks.
