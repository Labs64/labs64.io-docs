# Labs64.IO Services & Modules

Core Platform: Auth Gateway authenticates requests and enforces policy; AuditFlow routes tenant-aware audit events to configured sinks; MCP Gateway is planned for governed AI-tool access.

Commerce & Billing: Payment Gateway normalizes payment-provider interactions; Checkout manages purchase orders, customers and checkout transactions; NetLicensing and Invoicing are planned for entitlement and billing documents.

Extensions: Reporting answers read-only queries over the events AuditFlow's sinks stored, each customer's own rows only; Customer Portal is a front-end shell for a customer-facing app; Notifications and GuideChimp are planned.

Planned services describe their intended design only and must not be treated as available APIs. Complete pages are linked from /docs/services/.
