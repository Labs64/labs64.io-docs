# Deployment Tiers

Choose the operating model that matches your team's desired level of infrastructure ownership. The exact commercial packaging and service availability are agreed separately; the tiers below describe the intended operational shape.

| Tier | You operate | Labs64.IO role | Best fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community Edition | Your cluster, integrations, upgrades, and support process | Public software and documentation | Teams evaluating or self-supporting |
| Managed SaaS | Minimal platform infrastructure | Operates the agreed managed environment | Teams focused on product delivery |
| Assisted Enterprise | Your environment with shared operational responsibility | Guidance, enablement, and agreed support | Regulated or enterprise-owned platforms |
| Bespoke | A tailored environment and integration model | Solution design and delivery by agreement | Specialized constraints or integrations |

Every tier benefits from the same foundations: explicit identity, secret management, tenant boundaries, backups, observability, and an incident response path.
