# Reporting

> **Planned service**
>
> Reporting is a forward-looking capability description. Reporting APIs, datasets, and deployment artifacts are not yet released.

Reporting will provide governed views of operational and business data for administrators and product teams. It is intended to consume approved service data rather than become a shared application database.

| Capability | Intended behavior |
|---|---|
| Curated datasets | Service-owned data exposed through supported integration paths |
| Tenant-aware views | Reports scoped to the current tenant and caller permissions |
| Scheduled output | Repeatable report delivery for operational routines |
| Operational insight | Connect service health and business activity when appropriate |
| Export controls | Controlled export suited to the report and its audience |

Early adopters should identify report audiences, data ownership, retention needs, and the entitlement rules that govern each report.
